BIM-BEM-BOM

Where the model meets the meter.

Open-source building energy modelling. BIM-BEM-BOM turns a building's IFC model into an EnergyPlus energy model, runs it, and compares the result with what the utility actually billed.

Download the preview View on GitHub

Free, Apache-2.0. Public preview for macOS, Windows and Linux.

Its name is its three parts

One program, carrying a building from the drawing to the meter.

BIMBuilding information model

Reads a building's IFC model: its rooms, walls, slabs, roofs, windows and doors. Each assembly is reduced to the single surface EnergyPlus needs, and anything it can't place is reported.

BEMBuilding energy model

Builds the energy model: site and weather, constructions, schedules, zones and systems. Then it runs EnergyPlus, which you install separately.

BOMBuilding operations model

Prices the simulated year with your utility's tariff, takes your bills and PV production, and calibrates the model against ASHRAE Guideline 14.

The building's measured operation is compared back against the model.

Practical and honest about what it knows

It reports what it can't place

When the program can't place something from the IFC, it says so, so you can see what the model left out.

It runs before you enter your own code

Public-domain reference values from the DOE/PNNL residential prototype models are included, for each IECC edition from 2006 to 2024.

You bring licensed data from its source

It ships no copyrighted standards tables, weather files or manufacturer data. You get each from its publisher, under its own terms.

Results are estimates

BIM-BEM-BOM produces simulation estimates, not code-compliance documentation. You verify the results you rely on.

Who it's for

Researchers, engineers, architects and anyone else interested in practical, honest building energy modelling. Early users are invited to try the preview, report what works and what doesn't, and help shape where it goes next.
